# Privacy Policy

**StimLife**
Last updated: March 26, 2026

---

## Summary

StimLife stores all data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or health data. There are no user accounts, no servers, and no analytics. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

## 2. What Data the App Accesses

### 2.1 Data You Enter Directly

StimLife stores the following information **on your device only**, using Apple's SwiftData framework in a local App Group container:

- Your medication type (e.g., Adderall IR, Vyvanse, Concerta)
- Your usual dose amount and time
- Your metabolism speed setting (Fast / Normal / Slow)
- The dates and times you log doses
- Optional free-text notes you attach to a logged dose

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server, not backed up to iCloud, and not shared with any third party.

### 2.2 HealthKit Data (Optional)

If you grant permission, StimLife may read the following data from Apple Health:

- **Sleep data** — to display sleep readiness context on the Now screen
- **Resting heart rate** — to display context alongside your medication curve

StimLife does **not** write any data to Apple Health.

HealthKit data is:
- Read only when the app is open and in use
- Never stored persistently by StimLife
- Never transmitted off your device
- Never used for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than displaying information to you in the app

You can revoke HealthKit permissions at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → StimLife**.

### 2.3 Notifications

StimLife schedules local notifications on your device to alert you when estimated medication activity is wearing off or has cleared. These notifications are processed entirely on your device. No notification data is transmitted to any server.

### 2.4 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

- Your name, email address, or any contact information
- Your location
- Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
- Crash logs or diagnostics (no analytics SDK is included)
- Usage data or behavioral data
- Any data transmitted to or from a server

---

## 3. How Data Is Used

All data accessed by StimLife is used **solely to display information to you within the app**. Specifically:

| Data | Used For |
|------|----------|
| Medication type & dose | Calculating your estimated pharmacokinetic curve |
| Dose log times | Determining current Focus Window state and time remaining |
| Metabolism speed | Adjusting the half-life estimate in the PK model |
| Sleep data (HealthKit) | Displaying sleep readiness context on the Now screen |
| Resting heart rate (HealthKit) | Displaying context alongside medication curve |

No data is used for analytics, advertising, marketing, research, or any purpose other than displaying information to you.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All StimLife data is stored in a local App Group container on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Data is protected by your device's standard iOS encryption (encrypted at rest when device is locked).

StimLife does not operate any servers. There is no cloud sync, no remote backup, and no network communication of any kind.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, disclose, or transfer your data to any third party, for any purpose, at any time.

There are no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services, or data brokers integrated into StimLife.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data persists on your device until you delete it. You can:

- Delete individual dose records from the **History tab** (swipe to delete)
- Delete your medication profile from the **Profile tab**
- Delete all app data by deleting the StimLife app from your device

When you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

StimLife is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. This app is intended for adults with a valid prescription for stimulant medication, under the care of a healthcare provider.

---

## 8. Important Medical Disclaimer

StimLife provides **educational estimates** of how stimulant medications typically metabolize, based on published pharmacokinetic research.

**StimLife is not a medical device. It is not approved or cleared by the FDA or any regulatory authority. It does not provide medical advice.**

Estimates shown are based on published general population pharmacokinetic data and are not clinically validated for any individual. Individual metabolism varies significantly based on genetics, body composition, kidney and liver function, diet, other medications, health conditions, and many other factors.

**Do not use StimLife to determine when or whether to take medication. Do not adjust your dose or medication regimen based on these estimates. Always consult your prescribing physician or pharmacist.**
